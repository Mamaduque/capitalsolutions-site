Privacy Notice for Business Contacts

We may have contacted you by email to offer our document organisation services to your firm. This notice explains what we hold about you and your rights (UK GDPR Article 14).

Who is responsible. Thiago Teixeira, trading as Capital Solutions, is the controller of this data. Contact details are above.

What we hold. Your name, job title, firm name, business email address, business phone number and business address. We do not collect personal home details or sensitive information.

Where we got it. From public sources: your firm's website, Companies House, and public professional directories (for example the SRA, ICAEW or ACCA registers, or property agent listings).

Why we use it. To send you short, relevant emails about our B2B document organisation services, and to keep a record of our contact with you.

Lawful basis. Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR): our interest in telling businesses about a service relevant to their work. See the assessment below. We email corporate addresses in line with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR); every email includes an easy way to opt out.

Who we share it with. Nobody, except our email provider (Google Workspace) which stores our emails. We do not sell or rent contact data.

How long we keep it. Up to 12 months after our last contact with you, then we delete it. If you opt out, we keep only your email address on a "do not contact" list so we never email you again.

Your rights.

  • Right to object: absolute. You can object to marketing at any time and we will stop. Just reply "STOP" to any email, or contact us.
  • You can also ask to see, correct or delete your data, or restrict how we use it.
  • You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

    • Transfers. Your contact details are held in the UK. Our email service providers may process data outside the UK; where they do, this is covered by appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy decision.

    Legitimate Interests Assessment (summary)

    Purpose: Capital Solutions wants to tell accountancy practices, law firms and letting agents about a document organisation service that helps their work; this is a genuine commercial interest. Necessity: a short, targeted email to a business address found on public sources is the least intrusive way to reach the right person; we use only business contact details and contact only firms whose work fits the service. Balancing: the people contacted are professionals acting in a business role, who would reasonably expect relevant B2B approaches at their published work address; the data is not sensitive; emails are low volume, clearly identify the sender, and every one offers a one-word opt-out ("STOP") that is honoured immediately and permanently; data is deleted after 12 months without contact. On balance, the individuals' interests do not override ours, so legitimate interests applies. Reviewed: 16 November 2026.
